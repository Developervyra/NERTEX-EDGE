NERTEX EDGE

Efficient AI Computing Research

NERTEX EDGE is an independent student-led research initiative exploring ways to improve AI computing efficiency through research, experimentation, and reproducible benchmarking.

Research Focus

- Efficient AI inference
- Model optimization and quantization
- Memory and computational efficiency
- Performance on resource-constrained hardware

Primary Research Question

Can we improve the inference efficiency of small AI models while preserving their predictive accuracy?

Research Principles

1. Evidence before claims.
2. Reproducible experiments.
3. Transparent benchmark methodology.
4. Honest reporting of limitations.
5. Open research with a carefully protected technology core.

Current Status

Research planning and baseline development.

No performance improvements are claimed at this stage.
