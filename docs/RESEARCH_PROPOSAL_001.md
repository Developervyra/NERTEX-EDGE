Research Proposal 001

Title: Improving AI Inference Efficiency on Resource-Constrained Hardware

Research ID: NER-EDGE-RP-001
Organization: NERTEX Research & Innovation
Project: NERTEX EDGE
Status: Proposed — Research Not Yet Validated

---

1. Abstract

Artificial intelligence models require computational resources to perform inference. These requirements can limit their use on devices with restricted processing power, memory, and energy availability.

This research will investigate methods for improving the inference efficiency of small AI models while preserving their predictive accuracy.

The project will examine model optimization techniques, establish reproducible performance measurements, and evaluate the trade-offs between computational efficiency and model quality.

2. Problem Statement

Many AI applications depend on hardware with substantial computational capabilities. However, resource-constrained devices have limited memory and processing capacity.

The research problem is to determine whether suitable optimization techniques can reduce inference time and memory requirements without causing unacceptable reductions in predictive accuracy.

3. Primary Research Question

Can the inference efficiency of small AI models be improved on resource-constrained hardware while preserving predictive accuracy?

4. Research Objectives

1. Study existing AI inference optimization techniques.
2. Establish a measurable baseline using a selected model.
3. Investigate quantization and other suitable optimization methods.
4. Compare optimized models against the original baseline.
5. Document performance, accuracy, memory usage, and limitations.
6. Develop a reproducible experimental prototype.

5. Research Scope

Initial research will focus on:

- Small machine learning models.
- CPU-based inference.
- Model quantization.
- Computational and memory efficiency.
- Accuracy and latency trade-offs.

Large-scale AI training and specialized hardware development are outside the initial research scope.

6. Proposed Methodology

The research will follow these stages:

1. Literature review.
2. Model and dataset selection.
3. Baseline implementation.
4. Baseline performance measurement.
5. Application of selected optimization techniques.
6. Comparative evaluation.
7. Reproducibility testing.
8. Research documentation.

7. Evaluation Metrics

The experiments will examine:

- Inference latency.
- Memory consumption.
- Model size.
- Predictive accuracy.
- Energy consumption, only if reliable measurement is available.

All comparisons will use documented hardware and software configurations.

8. Expected Outcomes

The expected deliverables are:

- A documented research methodology.
- A reproducible baseline implementation.
- An experimental optimization prototype.
- Comparative benchmark results.
- A technical research report.

These are intended outcomes, not completed achievements.

9. Limitations

Results may vary depending on hardware, model architecture, datasets, software libraries, and experimental conditions.

No specific performance improvement is guaranteed.

10. Research Integrity

NERTEX EDGE will prioritize reproducibility, transparent reporting, and evidence-based conclusions.

Failed experiments and negative results will also be documented.

11. Current Research Status
